Google Calendar Connection

Connect your own Google account to view Google Calendar events in this planner. This is separate from your ChatGPT connection. Gmail is never requested.

Setup for a deployed website: In Google Cloud Console, enable the Google Calendar API, configure an OAuth consent screen, and create a Web application OAuth client. Add your website's HTTPS origin under Authorized JavaScript origins. Paste the client ID below. For local testing, localhost may be authorized. Google requires a properly configured consent screen and may require verification for broader use.
